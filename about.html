Academic & Professional Journey

About Me

My development philosophy combines technical rigor with security-minded discipline. From writing clean, object-oriented Java and modern Laravel backends to conducting automated web vulnerability assessments with Nikto, I ensure systems are resilient, responsive, and maintainable.

As an active member of the SLIIT Computing Society, I am deeply involved in peer mentorship and academic research. I authored the empirical research study "The Impact of Gaming on IT Student Performance", applying R statistical computing to model correlation matrices between recreational habits and cognitive academic metrics.

In parallel, I spearheaded our campus community blood donation initiative, "Praana 2026", conducted on the 21st of October 2026 in collaboration with the Leo Club of SLIIT City University (SCU). Mobilizing over 40 voluntary donors across corporate office staff, university faculty, management personnel, and student batches, the campaign collected over 40 units of blood, demonstrating that technical leadership is most impactful when driven by community empathy.
