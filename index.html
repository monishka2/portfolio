Praana 2026: Community Blood Donation Campaign

"Praana 2026" was a community-driven health and social responsibility initiative organized in collaboration with the Leo Club of SLIIT City University (SCU), conducted on the 21st of October 2026. Aimed at supporting national healthcare needs by encouraging voluntary blood donations, the campaign served as a bridge between the university and the surrounding community, successfully raising health awareness and fostering a strong culture of giving.
