Praana 2026: Comprehensive CSR Case Study Report

1. Executive Summary & Overview

"Praana 2026" was a community-driven health and social responsibility initiative aimed at supporting national healthcare needs by encouraging voluntary blood donations. Conducted on the 21st of October 2026 at SLIIT City University, the campaign was organized in close collaboration with the Leo Club of SLIIT City University (SCU). The initiative served as a crucial bridge between the university and the surrounding community, successfully raising health awareness and fostering a strong culture of giving.

2. Target Participants & Reach

The event was intentionally open to a wide cross-section of the community, creating high engagement and meaningful participation across multiple distinct groups:

Corporate & Office Staff

Office staff and employees from surrounding corporate offices and commercial businesses surrounding the university.

Lecturers & Academic Staff

University lecturers, department heads, and academic mentors actively supporting the drive as donors and advocates.

University Management

University administrative management personnel providing institutional backing, logistical clearance, and personal participation.

Student Volunteers & Donors

Student volunteers and voluntary donors mobilised across various undergraduate batches and computing disciplines.

3. Key Achievements & Community Impact

  • Over 40 Blood Donors: With the collective, enthusiastic participation of donors, volunteers, and event organizers, the campaign successfully collected over 40 units of blood.
  • Community Healthcare Support: Directly contributed critical reserves to healthcare networks while raising vital awareness on the life-saving importance of voluntary blood donation.
  • Successful Team Execution: Exemplified strong inter-organizational collaboration and leadership between the student project team and the Leo Club of SLIIT City University (SCU).

4. Challenges Faced & Problem-Solving Tactics

Corporate & Academic Scheduling

Engaging external corporate office staff alongside university lecture schedules required flexible time slots and rapid pre-screening to respect busy work hours.

Pre-Donation Eligibility & Care

Volunteers with low hemoglobin or recent medications were counseled with positive nutritional advice and reassigned to cheer and assist donor reception.

Inter-Club Coordination

Aligning roles between the project team and the Leo Club of SCU was handled through pre-event operational matrices and designated zone leads.

5. Personal Reflection & Leadership Growth

Orchestrating "Praana 2026" was a transformative milestone in my academic and professional development. Collaborating closely with the Leo Club of SLIIT City University (SCU) demonstrated that technical engineering capability is at its best when driven by community empathy and civic duty. Leading cross-organizational teams, managing stakeholder communication across surrounding corporate offices, faculty, and student batches, and achieving over 40 units of life-saving blood reinforced the critical importance of adaptable leadership, crisis planning, and collective purpose.
